About SilverAssist

SilverAssist is a student-led project developed by a team from the University of Pretoria, South Africa. Our mission is to empower older adults with essential digital skills and information literacy, enabling them to navigate the digital world safely, confidently, and independently.

Our Mission

We aim to make technology more accessible and less intimidating by providing simple, easy-to-understand guidance on everyday digital tasks, online safety, and identifying potential scams. Through SilverAssist, we strive to help older adults build their digital confidence while staying connected, informed, and protected in an increasingly digital world.

Our commitment to accessibility

Accessibility is at the heart of our design. The platform follows the principles of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) to ensure that content is clear, perceivable, understandable, and easy to navigate. By applying accessibility-focused design principles, SilverAssist aims to create an inclusive digital experience that supports older adults in becoming more confident, independent, and informed digital users.

Digital Skills

Downloadable Apps

All apps must first be downloaded from the Apple App Store (iPhones) or Google Play Store (Android phones).

  1. 1

    Find and click on the Apple App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android phones) on your home screen that displays all apps.

  2. 2

    Once inside the app store, click on the search icon.

  3. 3

    Once the cursor and the keyboard appear, type in the desired app's name and press Enter.

  4. 4

    The desired app will appear among a list; click the desired app's image.

  5. 5

    The app will be shown with a short description and images; find the download icon and click on it.

  6. 6

    Accept any terms and conditions that may appear, or verify by putting your password into the window that pops up asking for verification.

  7. 7

    The app will then begin to download, and once fully downloaded, it will appear with a tick where the download icon used to be.

  8. 8

    You may now exit the app store and look for the desired app on the home screen, as it will be automatically placed there.

Steps may vary slightly depending on your phone's operating system version.

How to Use WhatsApp

WhatsApp is a free app used to call, send messages, photos, and voice notes over the internet.

  1. 1

    Download WhatsApp as per the previous instructions.

  2. 2

    The first thing that will appear is that WhatsApp will want to connect the app to your cell phone number; follow the easy instructions that appear.

  3. 3

    Once you put in your cell phone number, they will present options to verify that it is the correct number through either calling the phone or using an SMS OTP. Choose the desired option and connect the number to the app.

  4. 4

    You should be presented with the home page of WhatsApp.

  5. 5

    To begin messaging your contacts, tap the message icon labelled "Chats" at the bottom panel of your screen.

  6. 6

    Search for the name of the contact you want to message by typing their saved contact name in the search bar at the top of the page. E.g. "Nurse Jane".

  7. 7

    Tap on the desired contact and then type your message into the text box at the bottom of the screen.

  8. 8

    Tap the green arrow at the end of the text box to send the message.

  9. 9

    Once done messaging, exit the contact page by pressing the back arrow on the top left-hand side next to the contact's name.

Tip: to send a voice note, press and hold the microphone icon in the text box; it will begin recording and show a voice bar above the text box. Once done speaking, let go of the icon and press the green send button to send it, or the trash bin to delete it.
Tip: to call your desired contact, follow the same process as messaging, but instead of typing a message, press the phone icon at the top right-hand side of the screen, in line with the contact's name.

Writing & Sending Gmail

Gmail is an online mailing service where a person can send messages, files, and more to others' email accounts. There are other services like iCloud or Yahoo Mail, but these instructions use Gmail. We'll start by creating an account — if you already have a Gmail account, skip ahead to "Writing and sending emails in Gmail" below.

Creating a Google account

  1. 1

    Go to your phone's web browser (Safari or Google) and type "Google sign in" into the search bar.

  2. 2

    To create an account on Google, tap "Create account" and choose "For my personal use".

  3. 3

    Type in your name as it asks you to, then tap Next.

  4. 4

    Now create a Gmail address that will become your email address; you may pick from the suggested ones or create your own that fits the guidelines shown on screen.

  5. 5

    Once you have chosen your email address, create a strong password and type it twice. Make sure you can remember it and that it fits the guidelines shown on screen, e.g. using special characters like "!".

  6. 6

    Follow the rest of the easy steps, like adding a recovery phone number. Once all steps are complete, the screen will show that you have successfully created a Google account and email.

Write your email address and password down somewhere you'll be able to find them, and do not share this information with anyone you do not trust.

Writing and sending emails in Gmail

  1. 1

    Download the Gmail app as per our previous instructions, or go to gmail.com and log in using the email address and password you created.

  2. 2

    Tap on the pencil icon to start writing an email.

  3. 3

    An email text box will appear; begin in the "To" box by writing in the email address of the person you want to receive the email.

  4. 4

    Next, go to the subject box and type a short subject describing what your email is about.

  5. 5

    In the large empty text box, begin writing your message. You can add images, documents, or links by tapping the relevant icons at the bottom of the text box.

  6. 6

    Once you are done, tap "Send" and it will immediately go to the recipient. Make sure you're connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data to send the email.

  7. 7

    To view a sent email, tap the menu icon (three lines) at the top of the screen, then tap Sent. This takes you to a page showing all your sent emails.

Digital Skills

How to Use Google Search

Google is a search engine platform, meaning that on this app or website you can search for information or find other websites.

  1. 1

    Open the Google app, or go to the Google website through your phone's browser by searching "Google".

  2. 2

    At the top of the screen, you will see a search bar. Type what you wish to search for, then tap Enter or the search icon.

  3. 3

    Wait for the results to come up and scroll through the search results that are shown.

  4. 4

    Once you see the result that best matches your search, tap on the blue underlined words for that result. This should take you to the website with your desired information.

How to Use Facebook

Facebook is an online social media platform that lets you have a profile page, add friends, and share messages and photos to keep your loved ones and friends updated.

Creating your account

  1. 1

    First, download the Facebook app as per the instructions above.

  2. 2

    Tap Create New Account, or sign in to your existing account if you already have one.

  3. 3

    Enter your name, email address or number, password, date of birth, and gender.

  4. 4

    Tap Sign Up.

  5. 5

    Verify your account details by entering the OTP pin sent to your email or number — your account is now set up for use.

Finding and adding friends

  1. 1

    Tap the search bar at the top of the screen and type in the name of the person you are looking for.

  2. 2

    From the displayed options, pick the correct person's profile and tap Add Friend next to their profile picture.

  3. 3

    Wait for the person to accept your friend request — once they do, you'll be able to see each other's posts.

  4. 4

    To accept friend requests, go to the friends icon at the top of the home page, then accept a request by tapping Confirm next to the profile you wish to accept.

  5. 5

    To post on Facebook, tap the box that says "What's on your mind" at the top of your feed. On the new page, type your message or add photos, then tap Post.
